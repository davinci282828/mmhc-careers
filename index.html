Why MMHC

Why Build Your Career Here?

1

Work on something that matters.

Help make thoughtful, insurance-covered therapy easier to access. Whether you provide care, improve operations or build technology, your work contributes to that goal.

2

Bring ideas. Take responsibility.

We’re looking for people who notice what could work better and help improve it. Bring your judgment, take ownership of your work and turn good ideas into practical results.

3

Keep getting better.

Clinicians develop through supervision, peer consultation and training. Across every team, we value curiosity, thoughtful feedback and the drive to improve your craft.

Who We Look For

Ambition With Purpose.

We look for people who want to become excellent at what they do and take on meaningful responsibility. You ask good questions, act on feedback and follow through. As we grow, help us build an organization that gets better with every step.
